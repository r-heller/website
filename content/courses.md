## R Course

A four-course biostatistics programme in R and Quarto: foundations and core tests; regression, ANOVA and model diagnostics; study design, longitudinal data and causal inference; and modern statistical learning on high-dimensional biomedical data. A structured starting point that goes well beyond the basics.

[CTTIR.github.io/courses](https://CTTIR.github.io/courses/)

## Clinical Data Science in R · Course

Eight workshops (32 teaching units) on clinical AI and translational trauma research: diagnostic accuracy, grouped validation, longitudinal and mechanistic models, simulation, regulation, data protection and incident response — with R labs on synthetic data and a project-based assessment. Companion book: [Clinical Data Science in R](https://cttir.github.io/clinical-data-science-in-r/).

[cttir.github.io/clinical-data-science-in-r-course](https://cttir.github.io/clinical-data-science-in-r-course/)

## Game Theory Course

#equilibria: a course-style teaching site on game theory, decision-making and simulation in R, with more than 240 pages in 32 topic areas and an interactive Nash-equilibrium explorer.

[r-heller.github.io/equilibria](https://r-heller.github.io/equilibria/)
