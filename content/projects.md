A selection of larger, multi-repo projects I've built or contribute to.

## CTTIR

Reproducible computational tools, analyses, and teaching resources for trauma and tissue injury research — open-source software, analysis pipelines, and educational materials at the interface of trauma surgery, tissue injury, and computational biomedicine.

- GitHub: <https://github.com/CTTIR>
- Website: <https://cttir.github.io/website/>

## Fonderie

AI model engineering for scientific work: training and fine-tuning open models on domain data, inspecting and editing model weights, building customised local models, and evaluating them reproducibly on scientific tasks.

Early stage: the three tools below are scaffolds that state their scope; nothing has been released yet.

- GitHub: <https://github.com/fonderie-dev>
- Website: <https://fonderie-dev.github.io/website/>
- Training and fine-tuning: [Affinage](https://github.com/fonderie-dev/fonderie-affinage)
- Weight inspection and editing: [Burin](https://github.com/fonderie-dev/fonderie-burin)
- Reproducible evaluation: [Essai](https://github.com/fonderie-dev/fonderie-essai)

## Établi

A suite of tools for research, local knowledge work, games and self-hosted services. Projects include R and Python workspaces, Shiny apps, a research library, focus timers, document and project clients, and experimental board games. Each repository documents its current features and development builds.

- GitHub and project overview: <https://github.com/etabli-dev>
- Research workspace: [Atelier](https://github.com/etabli-dev/etabli-atelier)
- Research library: [Recueil](https://github.com/etabli-dev/etabli-recueil)
- Focus and task planning: [Focus](https://github.com/etabli-dev/etabli-focus)

## boulingua

A multilingual teaching platform providing educational resources for teaching English (EFL), French (FLE), and German (DaF) as foreign languages, organised as four Quarto-based sister sites authored by S. Le Boulanger.

- GitHub: <https://github.com/boulingua>
- Website: <https://boulingua.github.io/website/>
